Day by day

The complete 7-day plan

✈️ 🏛️ 🪨
Day 1

Colorado Springs Highlights

  • U.S. Air Force Academy
  • U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Museum
  • Garden of the Gods near sunset
🚙 ⛰️
Day 2

Pikes Peak Drive

Drive instead of taking the Cog Railway for flexibility, scenic pullouts, and better family value.

Tip: bring layers for the 14,115-ft summit.

🌉 🚠
Day 3

Royal Gorge

  • Royal Gorge Bridge
  • Aerial Gondola
  • Canyon viewpoints

Budget tip: the train is optional if the family fare is high.

🚗 🏞️ ⛰️
Day 4

Colorado Springs → Aspen

  • Buena Vista lunch / coffee
  • Twin Lakes photos
  • Independence Pass summit
  • Short Aspen stop
🌄 📸
Day 4 Evening

Maroon Bells

Evening trailhead parking makes the visit simple: drive in, walk a few minutes to Maroon Lake, enjoy the iconic reflection view, and continue to Glenwood Springs.

♨️ 🌊
Day 5

Glenwood Springs

  • Glenwood Hot Springs late afternoon / evening
  • Grand Avenue pedestrian bridge
  • Two Rivers Park
  • Downtown / riverfront
🏘️ 🌲 🌸
Day 6

Vail

Walk Lionshead → Gore Creek → Vail Village → Covered Bridge → Betty Ford Alpine Gardens.

About 3.5–4 miles round trip.

🏙️ 🎬 ✈️
Day 7

Vail → Denver

Keep the last day flexible with Golden, Lookout Mountain, Denver, or a movie before a late flight.

What we learned

Spend on the experiences that matter

Worth paying for

  • Pikes Peak drive
  • Royal Gorge Bridge + Gondola
  • Maroon Bells reservation
  • Glenwood Hot Springs

Easy to skip if budget matters

  • Royal Gorge train at high family pricing
  • Multiple gondolas / scenic train rides
  • Expensive Aspen overnight stay
  • Too many paid attractions in Glenwood
